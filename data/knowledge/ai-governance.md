# AI Governance Standard

## Pre-deployment controls
Before an AI model is deployed, the owner must document its intended use, data sources, limitations, evaluation results, and accountable approver. High-impact systems require a privacy assessment, security review, bias testing, human-oversight plan, and rollback procedure.

## Data protection
Personal data must be minimized, processed for a documented purpose, and protected through role-based access. Sensitive information must not be included in prompts unless the approved architecture and provider agreement support that data class.

## Human oversight
AI recommendations that can materially affect customers or employees require meaningful human review. The reviewer must be able to inspect supporting evidence, override the output, and record the final decision.

## Review cycle
Production AI systems are reviewed at least quarterly and whenever the model, data source, intended purpose, or risk classification changes.
